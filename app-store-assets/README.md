## App Store Connect Setup

### 1. Create App

- Go to App Store Connect
- Click "My Apps" → "+" → "New App"
- Fill in basic information

### 2. App Information

- **Bundle ID**: com.shabbospdf.weather
- **SKU**: shabbospdf-weather-ios
- **User Access**: Full Access

### 3. Pricing and Availability

- **Price**: Free
- **Availability**: All countries
- **Release Type**: Manual

### 4. App Review Information

- **Contact Information**: Your email
- **Demo Account**: Not required
- **Notes**: "This app provides weather forecasts and candle lighting times for Jewish Shabbos preparation. It uses location services to provide accurate local information."

## Privacy Requirements

### Privacy Policy

- Required for all apps
- Must be accessible via URL
- Should cover data collection and usage

### App Privacy Details

- **Data Used to Track You**: None
- **Data Linked to You**: None
- **Data Not Linked to You**: Location (for weather/times)

## Publishing Steps

1. **Get Apple Developer Account** ($99/year)
2. **Create App in App Store Connect**
3. **Upload iOS build** (via EAS or Xcode)
4. **Fill in App Store listing**
5. **Upload screenshots and app icon**
6. **Set up privacy policy**
7. **Submit for Review** (1-7 days typically)

## Notes

- iOS apps typically review faster than Android
- Apple is strict about privacy and app quality
- Ensure all permissions are properly explained
- Test thoroughly on multiple iOS devices
- Consider creating an App Store preview video

## Common Rejection Reasons

- Missing privacy policy
- Incomplete app information
- Poor app icon quality
- Missing screenshots
- App crashes during review
- Unclear app purpose
